The generated module preserves selected screenshot-app groups, including the
upstream global ad-alliance group, and omits unrelated app groups. No
Xiaohongshu-specific hostname, MITM target, rewrite, or script is included.
DOMAIN,dig.bdurl.net,REJECT is deliberately omitted because it breaks Douyin
Selection video playback on the user's device. The source revision and SHA-256
are recorded in sources.json. Upstream script URLs are retained from the source
module.

Surge module argument defaults were checked against the upstream Bilibili and
NetEase Cloud Music split modules at the same Wool Scripts revision. The local
sponsorBlock default is false so creator-sponsorship segments remain intact.

Candidate domains recorded for review, but not enabled in the generated module,
are derived from abclq/home-dns-adblock:
  Repository: https://github.com/abclq/home-dns-adblock
  File: config/adblock-fq.conf
  License: MIT
  Revision and selected exact hosts are recorded in sources.json.
