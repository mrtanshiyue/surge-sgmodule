Remote script resources
-----------------------

The module references remote script resources from upstream URLs; it does not bundle their JavaScript source. Those resources remain subject to the terms set by their respective authors and hosting repositories. The generated module records the currently selected script paths in PersonalAds.sgmodule; the source module inputs and their hashes are in sources.json.
